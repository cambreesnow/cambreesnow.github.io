Why Dance Matters

  1. Benefits
    • Confidence
    • Strength
    • Discipline
    • Leadership
    • Consistency
Dance has transformed me into the person I am today. Without dance I wouldn't understand discipline and what it takes to be successful. Dance is challenging but it is also so rewarding. When things are hard I have learned to problem solve instead of giving up. Dance is such a big part of my identity and I am so thankful for all it has taught me. I have also gained so many best friends because of dance. I am so thankful for the relationships and connections dance has given me.
