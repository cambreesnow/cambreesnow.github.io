About Me

My name is Cambree Lynn Snow and I am from Spanish Fork, Utah. I am currently 19 years old and in my Sophomore Year here at BYU. I started dancing at the age of 2 and have been in love with it ever since. I have wanted to go to BYU since I was a little girl. The reason for this wasn't because of a specific business school but instead because of the BYU Cougarettes. When I first saw the Cougarettes dance I decided that I had to be one of them someday. It took a lot of dedication and hard work but I made it and now I am here living my life to the fullest. I am a very passionate person. I care about those around me and always strive to have a positive mindset in all that I do. I don't want to be remembered by awards I won or degrees I got but instead I want to be remember as a kind human that helped people to feel loved and cared about. Some other hobbies of mine are watching a good show or movie, hanging out with my friends, spending time with family, or taking naps. I don't quite have a plan for my future yet but I find comfort in knowing God will help lead me in the direction I am meant to go.
